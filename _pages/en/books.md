## Selected Books

Academician Wang Chengshan has published 18 monographs and textbooks in Chinese and English. Representative works include *Continental Scientific Drilling Project of the Cretaceous Songliao Basin (SK-1 Well)*, *Principles and Methods of Sedimentary Basin Analysis*, and *Selected Works of Academician Wang Chengshan*.
