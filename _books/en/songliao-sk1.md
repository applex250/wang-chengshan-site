This book systematically summarizes the scientific achievements of the "Well SK-1" continental scientific drilling project: building on the "Golden Column", the world's most complete Cretaceous terrestrial geological record, it reconstructs the evolution of Cretaceous terrestrial palaeoclimate and palaeoenvironment, providing key scientific evidence for studying the greenhouse Earth climate system and the formation settings of oil and gas resources. The project was led by Academician Wang Chengshan as Chief Scientist and was awarded the First Prize of the National Science and Technology Progress Award in 2025.
