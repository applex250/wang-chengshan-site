One of the publication series commemorating the 70th anniversary of the founding of China University of Geosciences, this volume brings together Academician Wang Chengshan's representative academic papers and scientific essays in the fields of the uplift of the Qinghai–Tibet Plateau, deep-time palaeoclimate and palaeoceanography, and continental scientific drilling, recording the four decades of scientific research of a sedimentologist.
