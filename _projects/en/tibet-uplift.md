Focusing on the frontier problem of the **uplift of the Qinghai–Tibet Plateau**, this research integrates sedimentology, palaeomagnetism and tectonics to systematically constrain the early uplift history of the plateau and its resource and environmental effects.

Since 1978, Academician Wang Chengshan has carried out field geological work in the Changdu (Qamdo) area of Tibet and around Yulong Snow Mountain, taking the plateau — a "natural geological laboratory" — as the starting point of his scientific career. The related series of achievements was awarded the **Second Prize of the 2015 National Natural Science Award**.
