As **Chief Scientist**, Academician Wang Chengshan led the 20-year International Continental Scientific Drilling Program (ICDP) project — the "International Continental Scientific Drilling Project of the Songliao Basin" — which was awarded the **First Prize of the 2025 National Science and Technology Progress Award**.

Drilling of the project began in 2006 (Well SK-1), with participation from more than 40 institutions and over a thousand researchers, and completed drilling of Wells SK-1 and SK-2:

- **Well SK-2** reached a total depth of **7,018 metres**, the deepest continental scientific drilling well in Asia;
- Continuous Cretaceous cores were recovered, with a **core recovery rate of over 96%**, building the world's first and most complete Cretaceous terrestrial geological section — the "**Golden Column**";
- Established international standards for high-resolution geochronological frameworks and palaeoclimate methodology, clarified the multi-timescale variability of Cretaceous terrestrial climate, confirmed the driving role of carbon dioxide concentration in greenhouse climates, and proposed new insights into the two-stage extinction of dinosaurs;
- Provided important scientific support for the exploration and development of deep oil and gas resources in the Songliao Basin (e.g., Daqing Gulong shale oil).

Academician Wang Chengshan is regarded by the ICDP as the Chief Scientist of a "lighthouse project", and the implementation of this project is hailed as an important milestone in China's "march into the deep Earth".
