Serves as leader of two United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) International Geoscience Programme (IGCP) projects, conducting international collaborative research on **deep-time palaeoclimate and palaeoceanography**.

The research covers topics such as the Cretaceous Oceanic Red Beds (CORB) and the evolution of the marine–terrestrial system during greenhouse climate intervals, promoting international exchange and cooperation in deep-time Earth system science.
