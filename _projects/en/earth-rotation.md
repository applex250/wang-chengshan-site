Supervises the **Big Data Sedimentology team** at Chengdu University of Technology, collaborating with world-leading institutions including the Paris Observatory (France), the Leibniz Institute for Applied Geophysics (Germany) and Trinity College Dublin (Ireland) on interdisciplinary research combining sedimentary big data with astronomical cycles.

The study published in the *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* (PNAS) in 2024 revealed the **staircase deceleration of Earth's rotation**, clarifying the intrinsic link between Earth's rotational rhythms and surface environmental change — a leap from fundamental geological research into the frontiers of astronomy.
