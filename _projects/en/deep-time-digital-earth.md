Academician Wang Chengshan initiated and serves as **Chair of the Executive Committee** of the **Deep-time Digital Earth (DDE) International Big Science Programme** — the **first International Big Science Programme** certified and launched by the International Union of Geological Sciences (IUGS).

Formally launched in 2019, the Programme is dedicated to integrating global data on Earth evolution and sharing geoscience knowledge worldwide, driving the paradigm shift of geoscience research toward a data-driven approach. Powered by big data, it aims to reconstruct the evolutionary history of Earth's life, geography, materials and climate, and to identify the macro-scale distribution patterns of global mineral and energy resources.

The DDE platform ([deep-time.org](https://deep-time.org)) integrates data, computing and knowledge, and has successively released flagship products including MyDDE, EarthExplorer, Web GPlates and GeoGPT. In December 2023, the internationally leading journal *Science* reported on the Programme across a full page.
