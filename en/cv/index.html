Profile

Born in Harbin, Heilongjiang Province, in November 1951, Prof. Wang Chengshan is a geologist and sedimentologist, an Academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences (elected in 2013), and a professor and doctoral supervisor at China University of Geosciences (Beijing). His research focuses on sedimentology and tectonics, with particular emphasis on the uplift of the Tibetan Plateau, deep-time paleoclimate and paleoceanography. He led the International Continental Scientific Drilling Project in the Songliao Basin and initiated the Deep-time Digital Earth (DDE) international big science program.

Research Interests

Tibetan Plateau Uplift and Sedimentary Response: Reconstruction of plateau uplift processes, sediment–tectonics coupling, and the "proto-Tibetan Plateau" uplift model
Deep-time Paleoclimate and Paleoceanography: Cretaceous oceanic red beds, greenhouse climates, continental scientific drilling, deep-time digital Earth
Sedimentology and Tectonics: Sedimentary basin analysis, Cretaceous continental scientific drilling in the Songliao Basin
Big-data Sedimentology: Cyclostratigraphy, Earth's rotation rhythms, and the intersection of astronomical cycles with sedimentary records
