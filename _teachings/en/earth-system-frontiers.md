## Course Introduction

Organized in the form of thematic seminars, this course tracks international frontiers in Earth system science, covering topics such as the deep-time digital Earth, cyclostratigraphy, and the coupling of Earth's rotation rhythms with climate and environment, while training doctoral students' ability to refine scientific questions and engage in international academic exchange.
