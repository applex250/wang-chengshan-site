## Course Introduction

Centring on the intersection of sedimentology and tectonics, this course draws on first-hand research cases such as scientific drilling in the Songliao Basin and the sedimentary records of the Qinghai–Tibet Plateau, cultivating graduate students' ability to decipher tectonic and climatic signals from sedimentary records.

## Main Content

- Principles and methods of sedimentary basin analysis
- Sediment–tectonic coupling and orogenic belt evolution
- Reconstruction of deep-time palaeoclimate and palaeoceanography
- Continental scientific drilling and sedimentary big data
