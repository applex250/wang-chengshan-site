title: Team Research Published in PNAS Reveals Stepwise Deceleration of Earths Rotation
languages: [en]
---

A research outcome led by the Big Data Sedimentology team of Chengdu University of Technology (guided by Academician Wang Chengshan), in collaboration with international institutions including the Paris Observatory (France), the Leibniz Institute for Applied Geophysics (Germany), and Trinity College Dublin (Ireland), was published in August 2024 in the *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* (PNAS). The study reveals a stepwise deceleration of Earth's rotation and reconstructs the evolution of this deceleration from 700 million to 200 million years ago (DOI: 10.1073/pnas.2317051121).
