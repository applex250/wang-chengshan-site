languages: [en]

On 3 October, the Himalaya 8.26 Extreme Geohazard Emergency Cross-border Scientific Expedition held its departure ceremony in Chengdu and set out for Nepal on 4 October for a joint survey of about 20 days. Academician Wang Chengshan serves as Commander-in-Chief and Chinese Chief Scientist, leading the team together with Academician Bishal Upreti of the Nepal Academy of Science and Technology. More than 50 researchers from over ten universities and institutes, including China University of Geosciences (Beijing), the Nepal Academy of Science and Technology, Chengdu University of Technology and Nanjing University, cover the source area in Nepal, the affected area in China and the downstream basin.
