As Chair of the Chinese Commission on Sedimentology and Vice President of the International Association of Sedimentologists, Academician Wang Chengshan led a delegation to the 22nd International Sedimentological Congress (Wellington, New Zealand), promoting the internationalization of "sedimentary-sphere science".
