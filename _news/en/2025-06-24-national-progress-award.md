title: International Continental Scientific Drilling Program in the Songliao Basin Wins the First Prize of the State Scientific and Technological Progress Award
languages: [en]
---

On June 24, 2025, the National Science and Technology Conference and the National Science and Technology Awards Ceremony were held in Beijing. The project "International Continental Scientific Drilling Program in the Songliao Basin: Innovation and Discovery", with Academician Wang Chengshan as chief scientist, won the First Prize of the 2025 State Scientific and Technological Progress Award.

The project accomplished the feat of "three wells, four boreholes, ten thousand meters of drilling, and eight thousand meters of continuous coring", setting records for the world's deepest borehole (7,018 m), the longest continuous drill core (8,186.96 m), and an ultra-high core recovery rate (over 96%). It penetrated the Cretaceous continental strata and established the world's first most complete "golden pillar" record of Cretaceous continental geology. In an interview, Academician Wang Chengshan said that the drilling achievements of the Chinese team "completed the historical archive of the entire Earth spanning about 4.6 billion years". (Reported by CCTV, China News Service, and others)
