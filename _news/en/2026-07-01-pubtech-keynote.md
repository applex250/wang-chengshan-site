In June 2026 (during the Beijing International Book Fair), Academician Wang Chengshan was invited to attend the 4th PubTech Publishing and Innovation Conference and delivered the **opening keynote** *Scientific Journals in the AI Era: Understanding and Practice*, systematically analyzing the impact of generative AI on academic publishing.

Academician Wang Chengshan currently serves as the **founding Editor-in-Chief** of the review journal *Earth Science Horizons* (ESH), co-founded by him together with the founder of the European Geosciences Union (EGU). In his report, he introduced the **TRAC academic principles** (Transparency, Reproducibility, Accountability, Clarity) first proposed by ESH, advocating their practice in academic publishing in the AI era.
