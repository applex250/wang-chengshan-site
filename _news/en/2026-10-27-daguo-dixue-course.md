languages: [en]

In the autumn semester of 2026, China University of Geosciences (Beijing) launched a new general education elective, Geoscience of a Great Nation (Autumn), taught jointly by eight academicians of the Chinese Academy of Sciences — Xu Yigang, Li Xianhua, Wang Chengshan, Hou Zengqian, Peng Jianbing, Zhang Peizhen, Zhao Guochun and Ding Lin. Eight lectures link the scientific picture from Earth's interior to the surface system, from deep-time evolution to the modern environment, and from resource security to deep-space exploration. The first lecture was held on the evening of 27 October and streamed live on the university's official video channel.
