On 21 September, Chengdu University of Technology held its 70th Anniversary Innovation and Development Conference in Chengdu, attended by representatives of 96 universities, research institutes and overseas partner institutions. Academician Wang Chengshan spoke as an alumni representative, recalling his student years at his alma mater and encouraging younger students to hold fast to their passion and shoulder their mission.
