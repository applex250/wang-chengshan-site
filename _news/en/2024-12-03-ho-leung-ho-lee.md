On December 3, 2024, the Ho Leung Ho Lee Foundation 2024 Award Ceremony was held at the Diaoyutai State Guesthouse in Beijing, where Academician Wang Chengshan received the Ho Leung Ho Lee Foundation Prize for Scientific and Technological Progress (Earth Sciences).
