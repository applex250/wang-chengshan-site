Academician Wang Chengshan joined the Tan Kah Kee Science Award Foundation in Guizhou for the "Tan Kah Kee Lecture" activities, giving popular science talks at schools including Guiyang Yali Senior High School, and attending a consultation forum where academicians offered advice to support the development of the manganese industry in Tongren.
