The International Symposium on Deep Earth Exploration and Asian 4D Topography (Deep & Topo 2026) opened in Beijing on 7 November, bringing together experts and scholars from many countries and regions to discuss Earth's internal structure and dynamic processes, the formation of mineral and energy resources, and earthquakes and geological hazards. The symposium was guided by the China Geological Survey and the National Natural Science Foundation of China and organised by the Chinese Academy of Geological Sciences, China University of Geosciences (Beijing), Chengdu University of Technology, the Geological Society of China and the Southern University of Science and Technology. Academicians Hou Zengqian and Wang Chengshan co-chaired its scientific committee.
