On February 26, 2019, the Deep-time Digital Earth (DDE) international big science program, initiated and led by Chinese scientists including Academician Wang Chengshan, was officially launched. It is the first international big science program certified by the International Union of Geological Sciences (IUGS), with Academician Wang Chengshan serving as Chair of the Executive Committee.
