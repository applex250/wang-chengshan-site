In July 2023, the Ministry of Education announced the winners of the 2022 National Teaching Achievement Awards. The project "Construction and Practice of a 'Five-in-One' System for Cultivating Top-Notch Geology Talent" of China University of Geosciences (Beijing) won the Second Prize of the National Teaching Achievement Award in Higher Education (Undergraduate), with Academician Wang Chengshan among its contributors.
