title: Chinese-Nepalese Joint Expedition Released First-Phase Survey Results and Completed a Full-Basin Remote Sensing Review of the 8.26 Cross-border Disaster
languages: [en]
---

On 13 October, the Himalaya 8.26 Extreme Geohazard Emergency Cross-border Scientific Expedition released its first-phase survey results. For the first time, a 0.5-metre-resolution remote sensing image map of the whole basin in the disaster-struck cross-border area was produced, overlaying pre- and post-disaster imagery on a single interpretation map and reconstructing the chain from the rock-ice avalanche source area in Nepal to the downstream basin. The team completed about 120 km of ground surveys along the affected Trishuli River basin in Nepal and helicopter checks of about 40 km of interrupted road below the Jilong Port, totalling 160 km of ground and aerial verification.
